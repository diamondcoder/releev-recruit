Our Story

Founded to Make a Difference

Founded with a vision to transform recruitment through specialization and quality, our company focuses on three key sectors: life sciences, engineering, and management.

With extensive experience in scientific research and industry collaboration, we understand the challenges organizations face when hiring highly skilled professionals. Generic recruitment just doesn't cut it in specialized fields.

That's why we built Releev Recruitment — a recruitment firm that speaks your language, understands your domain, and delivers candidates who genuinely fit.

🌍

Our Vision

To become a trusted recruitment partner across Europe, known for quality, expertise, and long-term relationships.

Our Mission

To connect exceptional talent with innovative organizations and contribute to advancements in science, technology, and business.
